PART 2 · STORY BANK

存钱买想要的东西

Describe a time when you saved money to buy something you wanted

已练 · 稳定二手摩托车

快速准备 · 关键词

campuslong distancemotorcycleexpensivesecond-handgood value

故事线

Huge campus -> dorm far from classrooms -> wanted motorcycle -> new too expensive -> second-hand marketplace -> reasonable price/good condition -> bought -> easier commute -> good value.

可串联题目

Saved money / Useful purchase / Shopping decision / Something worth buying

练过的答案

I am going to describe a time when I saved money to buy something I really wanted. When I was at university, my campus was huge, and it was inconvenient to walk from my dormitory to the classrooms every day. So I wanted to buy a motorcycle to make travelling around campus easier. However, a new one was too expensive for me as a student. Instead of buying a brand-new one, I decided to look on my university's second-hand marketplace. After searching for a while, I found a friendly seller who offered a reasonable price. The motorcycle was still in good condition, so I decided to buy it. After that, my daily life became much easier, and I also saved quite a lot of money. I felt very satisfied because it was useful, affordable and good value for money. This experience also showed me that buying second-hand can be a practical choice.

高价值纠错

  • second-hand market/place wording -> second-hand marketplace
  • price is reason -> reasonable price
  • condition is good -> in good condition
  • do not mix motorcycle and bicycle

主动词汇

second-hand marketplace, reasonable price, in good condition, good value for money, brand-new
