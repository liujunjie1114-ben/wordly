PART 2 · STORY BANK

喜欢的自然地点

Describe a natural place you like

已练 · 稳定长沙地标

快速准备 · 关键词

Yueluwestsubwayfreechildhoodpanoramic view

故事线

Yuelu Mountain -> west of Changsha -> university area -> subway/easy/free -> childhood with parents -> now with friends -> hike/camp -> city night view -> memories.

可串联题目

Natural place / Scenic spot / Place full of memories

练过的答案

When it comes to a natural place I like, the first place that comes to mind is Yuelu Mountain in Changsha. It is in the western part of the city, close to the university area, and it is one of the best-known scenic spots in Changsha. It is easy for everyone to get there because there is a subway station nearby, and entry is free. I have been going there since I was a child. When I was little, my parents often took me there to climb the mountain and exercise. Now I usually go with friends. Sometimes we hike, hang out or even camp nearby. What I enjoy most is the view from the top. When I reach the top, I can get a panoramic view of Changsha, and the night view is especially beautiful. Yuelu Mountain is not only a natural place for me; it is also full of personal memories.

高价值纠错

  • in our city's west -> in the western part of the city
  • will easy for everyone -> it is easy for everyone to get there
  • come into our eyes -> panoramic view / the whole city comes into view

主动词汇

western part of the city, scenic spot, at the foot of the mountain, panoramic view, personal memories
