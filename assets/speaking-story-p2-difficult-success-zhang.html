PART 2 · STORY BANK

克服困难并成功的人

Describe a person you know who did something difficult and was successful

已练 · 稳定设计暑校

快速准备 · 关键词

designercompanyfew clientspressurepersistencesuccess

故事线

Industrial designer -> started own company -> few clients + small team -> design + business pressure -> kept improving/listened to feedback -> reputation -> success.

可串联题目

Successful person / Difficult achievement / Persistent person

练过的答案

When it comes to a person I know who did something difficult and was successful, the first person that comes to mind is an industrial designer I met at a design summer school. During my internship at his company, he told me that starting his own design business was one of the hardest things he had ever done. At first, he had very few clients and a small team, so he had to deal with both design work and business pressure. However, he kept improving his work, listened to feedback and gradually built a good reputation. I really admire him because he was persistent and willing to learn from mistakes. His experience also made me more confident about my own career path.

高价值纠错

  • start company tense/control -> started his own company
  • success person -> successful person
  • focus challenge itself, not only how you met him

主动词汇

few clients, business pressure, listen to feedback, build a reputation, persistent
