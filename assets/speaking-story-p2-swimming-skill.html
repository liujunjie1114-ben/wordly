PART 2 · STORY BANK

小时候学的新技能

Describe a new skill you learned when you were a child

已练 · 稳定学游泳

快速准备 · 关键词

summerafraidfriendarms + legs + breathingpractiseproud

故事线

Summer vacation -> afraid of water -> friend taught -> arms/legs/breathing -> kept practising -> swim by myself -> proud -> practice makes perfect.

可串联题目

New skill / Difficult thing / Someone helped you / Achievement / Childhood experience

练过的答案

When I was a child, I learned how to swim during a summer vacation. It was a very memorable experience for me because swimming was quite difficult at the beginning. At first, I was really afraid of water, and I did not know how to control my body in the pool. I had some problems with my arm and leg movements, and I did not know how to breathe properly in the water. Sometimes water got into my mouth and nose, which made me feel very uncomfortable. At one point, I even wanted to give up. Luckily, one of my friends was very patient with me. She held my hand and taught me how to move my arms, kick my legs and breathe properly in the water. So instead of giving up, I just kept practising and followed her instructions. Gradually, I became more comfortable in the water. After practising for some time, I could finally swim by myself. At that moment, I felt extremely proud of myself because something that seemed very difficult at first became possible through practice. This experience taught me that practice really makes perfect.

高价值纠错

  • learned to swimming -> learned how to swim
  • afraid water -> afraid of water
  • patient for me -> patient with me
  • she teach me -> she taught me
  • breath -> breathe
  • swim myself -> swim by myself / on my own

主动词汇

at one point, be patient with somebody, follow her instructions, keep practising, on my own, float, practice makes perfect
