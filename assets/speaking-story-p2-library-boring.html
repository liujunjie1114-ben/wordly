PART 2 · STORY BANK

无聊的地方

Describe a boring place you have been to

已练 · 稳定大学图书馆

快速准备 · 关键词

librarylast week + classmateproject deadlinequiet + whisperpapers + sore eyesproductive but boring

故事线

University library -> last week -> classmate -> project deadline -> whole afternoon -> whispering / papers / sore eyes -> productive but frustrating.

可串联题目

Boring place / Quiet place / Place to study / Difficult study experience

练过的答案

The boring place I'd like to talk about is the library at my university. I went there last week with one of my classmates because we both had an important project deadline. We still had a lot of work to do, so we decided to spend the whole afternoon there. We arrived at around one o'clock after lunch and didn't leave until nearly six. To be honest, I wasn't really looking forward to going there because I already knew it would be long and tiring. The library itself is clean, quiet and actually a good place to study, but that's also the main reason I find it boring. Everyone has to keep quiet, so when I wanted to discuss my project with my classmate, we had to whisper. That was a little inconvenient. Another reason was that I spent almost the whole afternoon reading academic papers and staring at my laptop. After a while, my eyes felt sore and time seemed to pass really slowly. I couldn't relax by watching videos or chatting because I didn't want to disturb other people. The silence gradually became a bit stressful and frustrating. Of course, I have to admit that the library was productive because I finished most of my work that day. But if you ask me whether I enjoyed spending the whole afternoon there, definitely not. That's why it's one of the most boring places I've been to.

高价值纠错

  • I go there last week -> I went there last week
  • must have to whisper -> had to whisper / must whisper
  • feel not very relax -> did not feel relaxed
  • not freedom -> do not feel free / have very little freedom

主动词汇

deadline, the whole afternoon, whisper, inconvenient, productive, frustrating, sore eyes, disturb other people
