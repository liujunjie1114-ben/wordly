待补充：原资料尚无稳定的个人答案。请通过“补充本题”完善真实细节。

快速准备 · 关键词

designstudy abroadprofessional goalskillsstepsfuture

故事线

Final review retained three safe directions: design / study abroad / professional goal. Current Part 1 also uses AI + creative design as a future direction.

可串联题目

Ambition / Long-term goal / Future career

原文提纲与待补说明

Codex should present this as a selectable story shell, not a fixed autobiographical answer. The most consistent user-specific direction is industrial design + creative technology/AI, with study-abroad as an alternative if the user chooses it.

高价值纠错

  • realize my ambition / achieve my goal; do not say finish my achievement

realize my ambition, achieve my goal, career path, creative technology, long-term goal
