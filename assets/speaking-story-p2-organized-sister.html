PART 2 · STORY BANK

很有条理的人

Describe a person you know who is very organized

已练 · 稳定有条理的姐姐

快速准备 · 关键词

sisterto-do listdeadlinestidy roomprepare next dayrespect

故事线

Sister -> organized at work/home -> to-do list -> deadlines -> tidy room -> next-day prep -> saves time/reduces stress -> respect.

可串联题目

Organized person / Person you respect

练过的答案

When it comes to a person I know who is very organized, the first person that comes to mind is my sister. She is organized both at work and at home. At work, she makes a to-do list and plans her day carefully. She also checks deadlines, so she rarely forgets important tasks. At home, she keeps her room tidy and prepares things for the next day. I noticed this quality when we were growing up. I think being organized saves her time and reduces stress, and I really respect her for it.

高价值纠错

  • he/she -> keep she/her throughout
  • a organized -> an organized
  • in the job -> at work
  • I am really respect her -> I really respect her

主动词汇

to-do list, check deadlines, prepare for the next day, save time, reduce stress
