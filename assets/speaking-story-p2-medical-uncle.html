PART 2 · STORY BANK

医疗行业的人

Describe a person you know who works in the medical field

已练 · 稳定医生叔叔

快速准备 · 关键词

uncledoctorchildhoodsickpatientproud

故事线

Uncle -> local hospital doctor -> knew him since childhood -> went to see him when sick -> patient and calm -> respect / proud.

可串联题目

Medical worker / Doctor you know / Person you respect

练过的答案

The person I want to talk about is my uncle, who works as a doctor at a local hospital. I've known him all my life, and when I was younger, whenever I felt sick or had a headache, I would usually go to see him. He was always patient and calm, and he would explain what was wrong in a simple way and give me some medicine if I needed it. What I respect most is the way he treats his patients. Even when he is busy, he listens carefully and tries to make people feel comfortable. Being a doctor seems exhausting, both physically and mentally, so I really admire his attitude towards his work. I'm proud of him, and I feel lucky to have someone like him in my family.

高价值纠错

  • if I have an ill -> if I felt sick / if I was ill
  • ask for he help -> ask him for help
  • take medicine to me -> give me some medicine
  • proud to him -> proud of him

主动词汇

go to see a doctor, be patient with patients, medical treatment, proud of somebody, local hospital
