PART 2 · STORY BANK

喜欢或不喜欢的高楼

Describe a high-rise / tall building you like or dislike

已练 · 待核对大学图书馆

快速准备 · 关键词

university libraryabout 7 floorsglass exteriorreading roomsstudydeadlinetoo quiet

故事线

Reuse the university library. Shift focus from “boring afternoon” to building: around seven floors, glass exterior, modern, reading/study use, but too quiet and restrictive for discussion.

可串联题目

High-rise building / Building you dislike / Place you often visit

练过的答案

When it comes to a high-rise building I dislike, the first building that comes to mind is the main library at my university. If I remember correctly, it has around seven floors and much of the outside is covered with glass, so it looks quite modern. Most of the building is used for reading, studying and doing assignments, and I often use the reading rooms when I have a deadline. The problem is that I find the atmosphere too quiet. Everyone has to keep their voice down, so it is difficult to discuss a group project naturally. After sitting there for hours and staring at my laptop, I also start to feel tired and my eyes feel sore. I understand why a library needs to be quiet, but personally I do not enjoy staying there for a long time. That is why it is a building I use quite often but do not really like.

高价值纠错

  • the place is high -> it is a high-rise building
  • comes to my mind -> comes to mind
  • covered by glass -> covered with glass
  • must have to keep quiet -> have to keep quiet

主动词汇

high-rise building, covered with glass, reading room, keep your voice down, deadline
