PART 2 · STORY BANK

采访名人

Describe a time when you interviewed a famous person

已练 · 稳定设计暑校

快速准备 · 关键词

Mr Zhangsummer schoolnervousrelaxedinspirationsolve real problems

故事线

Mr Zhang -> well-known industrial designer -> summer design school -> nervous -> relaxed -> inspiration + early-career difficulties -> design solves real problems.

可串联题目

Famous person interview / Industrial designer / Meaningful conversation

练过的答案

When it comes to a famous person I have interviewed, the first person that comes to mind is Mr Zhang, a well-known industrial designer. I met him during a design summer school at a university. At the beginning, I was quite nervous because I had never interviewed such a well-known designer before. However, once we started talking, I found that he was kind, warm-hearted and easy-going, so I gradually felt more relaxed. During the interview, I asked him where he got his design inspiration and what difficulties he faced when he first entered the design field. What impressed me most was his view of good design. He told me that design is not only about making a product look attractive; more importantly, it should understand people's real needs and solve real problems in daily life. I found that idea very meaningful. After the interview, I felt more confident about studying industrial design and wanted to explore the field more deeply.

高价值纠错

  • industry design person -> industrial designer
  • he is kindness -> he was kind
  • fell relaxed -> felt relaxed
  • when he into this field -> when he first entered the field
  • design is only pretty -> design is not only about appearance

主动词汇

industrial designer, warm-hearted, easy-going, gradually feel relaxed, design inspiration, solve real problems
