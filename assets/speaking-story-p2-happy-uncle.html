PART 2 · STORY BANK

快乐的人

Describe a happy person you know

已练 · 稳定餐厅叔叔

快速准备 · 关键词

optimisticbright sidecheerful voicelaughsmall momentspositive energy

故事线

Same uncle, but shift evidence: optimistic -> bright side -> cheerful voice/laugh -> small moments -> shares positive energy.

可串联题目

Happy person / Positive person / Person who cheers others up

练过的答案

A happy person I know is my uncle, who runs a restaurant in my hometown. What makes him stand out is his positive attitude. No matter what happens, he usually tries to look on the bright side. Even when the restaurant is busy, he speaks to us in a cheerful voice and laughs a lot. I rarely see him stay in a negative mood for long. What I admire most is that he can find joy in small moments, such as having a nice meal, chatting with customers or spending time with family. He also likes to share positive energy and cheer other people up. So I think his happiness comes from the way he looks at life, rather than from something big or expensive.

高价值纠错

  • opposite -> optimistic
  • keep optimistic attitude -> stay optimistic
  • treat us a meal -> treat us to a nice meal
  • repeat happy too often -> show happiness through behaviour

主动词汇

look on the bright side, cheerful voice, find joy in small moments, share positive energy, cheer somebody up
