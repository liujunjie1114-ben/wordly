PART 2 · STORY BANK

改变决定

Describe a time when you changed a decision

待补个人细节改变决定

待补充：原资料尚无稳定的个人答案。请通过“补充本题”完善真实细节。

快速准备 · 关键词

initial choicereasonchanged mindnew choiceresultfeeling

故事线

Final review suggested using a study / design / purchase decision. Do not use generic question-bank major-change scripts as personal history unless confirmed.

可串联题目

Changed decision / Decision with result

原文提纲与待补说明

Transfer candidate: the summer-school decision can inspire the structure, but the cue requires an actual change from one decision to another. Codex should request/confirm a real initial decision and the new decision before finalizing.

高价值纠错

  • Do not answer only “important decision”; explicitly state what changed

主动词汇

change my mind, reconsider, weigh the pros and cons, right choice
