IELTS SPEAKING QUESTION BANK · PART 2
Course / Experience

Describe a course that impressed you a lot

上次课 Part 2 串题整理｜核心目标：1 分钟准备，1:40–2:00 稳定输出，不背整篇。

Cue Card

题卡四问

  • What the course was about
  • Where you took the course
  • What you did during the course
  • Explain why it impressed you a lot
1-minute Notes

只写 6 个关键词

summer school industrial design university lecture + projects basic electronics solve real problems
Story Route

你的固定故事线

① 定位课程：Last summer → short industrial design summer school。

② 地点与老师：在一所大学参加；Mr Zhang / a professor gave lectures。

③ 做了什么：听 lecture → team projects → learn basic electronics → make simple working prototypes。

④ 具体场景：有同学做 robot / analytical device；也有人做 healthcare-related products。

⑤ 为什么印象深：第一次真正意识到 design is not just about drawing or appearance; it is about solving real problems。

⑥ 收尾：课程让我更确定自己想继续做 industrial design，并尝试做更 useful / meaningful products。

Band 6.5 Safe Answer

稳定版答案

Last summer, I joined a short industrial design summer school at a university. The course was mainly about how designers can solve real problems through products, rather than simply making things look attractive.

At the beginning of the course, a professor called Mr Zhang gave us several lectures about design thinking and how to observe problems in everyday life. After that, we worked on some team projects. We also learned some basic electronics and tried to make simple working prototypes.

What impressed me most was seeing how different students turned their ideas into actual products. Some students made small robots or analytical devices, while others focused on healthcare-related products. I found that really interesting because everyone started from a different problem.

The biggest thing I learned was that design is not just about drawing or appearance. It is about problem-solving and making people's lives better. Since that course, I've become more interested in industrial design and more certain that I want to create useful products in the future. That's why the course left such a strong impression on me.
