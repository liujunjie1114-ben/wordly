PART 2 · STORY BANK

满意的重要决定

Describe an important decision you made and were happy with

已练 · 稳定设计暑校

快速准备 · 关键词

summer schoolhesitantlectureadvicestay in touchinternship

故事线

Summer school -> hesitant -> industrial-design exposure -> lecture -> talk with designer -> advice/contact -> internship -> happy with result.

可串联题目

Important decision / Decision with a good result / Opportunity

练过的答案

When it comes to an important decision I was happy with, the first thing that comes to mind is my decision to attend a design summer school last summer. At that time, I was a little hesitant because the programme would take up quite a lot of my summer break, and I also had other plans. However, I really wanted to learn more about industrial design and meet people working in the field, so in the end I decided to join. During the programme, I attended a lecture given by a well-known industrial designer. After the talk, I had the chance to speak with him in person. He shared a lot of his experience with me and gave me some valuable advice about my studies and future career. We added each other on WeChat, and he also gave me his email address. Later, we stayed in touch, and after a while he offered me an internship opportunity at his company. I've learned many practical things from that internship, so overall I'm really happy that I made that decision.

高价值纠错

  • I am hesitate -> I was hesitant
  • cost me too much time -> take up a lot of time
  • chatted with he -> chatted with him
  • shared me -> shared his experience with me
  • internship chance -> internship opportunity

主动词汇

be hesitant about, take up time, stay in touch, valuable advice, internship opportunity
