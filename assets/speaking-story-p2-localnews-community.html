PART 2 · STORY BANK

本地新闻

Describe a piece of local news that people were interested in

已练 · 待核对社区中心

快速准备 · 关键词

sister told menew community centrenear neighbourhoodlibrary + gymcoffee/workshoplocal residents

故事线

Sister told you -> a new community centre opened near the neighbourhood -> includes library, gym and coffee/workshop space -> you visited -> useful and convenient -> local residents interested.

可串联题目

Local news / Community facility / Useful place in the neighbourhood

练过的答案

A piece of local news I remember was about a new community centre opening near my neighbourhood. My sister told me about it first, and later I saw more information about it online. The centre included a small library, a gym and some shared spaces where people could have coffee or join workshops. I went there not long after it opened and spent some time reading and looking around. I think local residents were interested because it was not just a piece of news; it was something that could make daily life more convenient. Older people could use the facilities, students could study there, and families had a new place to spend time together. For me, it created a warmer and more connected feeling in the neighbourhood, so I remember the news quite clearly.

高价值纠错

  • she said me -> she told me
  • read for a book -> read a book
  • local peoples -> local residents
  • spend all day -> spend the whole day

主动词汇

local residents, community centre, shared space, convenient, a warm, connected feeling
